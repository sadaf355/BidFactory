# REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP)

# Government Cloud Modernization and Digital Services Platform

**RFP Reference:** GCS-2026-017  
**Issuing Organization:** Department of Digital Public Services (DDPS)  
**Issue Date:** 20 August 2026  
**Proposal Submission Deadline:** 30 September 2026  
**Document Classification:** Synthetic Demonstration RFP for the Bid Factory Academic Project

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION

The Department of Digital Public Services (DDPS) invites qualified technology vendors to submit proposals for the modernization of selected digital services, supporting applications, integration interfaces, data platforms, and associated operational capabilities.

The objective of this procurement is to identify a vendor capable of designing, implementing, migrating, securing, and supporting a modern cloud-based digital services environment.

The selected vendor will be expected to demonstrate relevant technical expertise, security controls, compliance evidence, government or public-sector delivery experience, implementation capability, and a commercially viable pricing approach.

---

# 2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The proposed solution should:

- Modernize selected legacy applications.
- Support secure cloud deployment.
- Enable integration between new and existing systems.
- Improve operational visibility and monitoring.
- Support secure handling of sensitive information.
- Provide scalable infrastructure and application architecture.
- Establish appropriate backup and disaster recovery capabilities.
- Provide implementation and post-production support.

---

# 3. MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS

The following requirements are mandatory unless otherwise stated.
