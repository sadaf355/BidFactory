# NovaTech Solutions — Service Level and Uptime Policy

Document ID: NTS-SUP-SLA-001
Classification: Customer Service Policy — Synthetic Demo Data
Version: 2.2

## Service Tiers
Standard: Business-hours support through approved email or portal channels.
Professional: Priority support with extended support coverage.
Enterprise: 24/7 incident support and enhanced service management.

## Enterprise Availability
For covered Enterprise Cloud Services, NovaTech provides a service target of 99.9% uptime, subject to the applicable service agreement and exclusions.

## Incident Prioritization
Incidents are prioritized based on severity and business impact.

## Response Commitments
Response and restoration targets depend on service tier, incident severity and contractual terms.

## Important Limitations
NovaTech does not provide a universal 99.99% uptime SLA as a standard offering.
NovaTech does not guarantee a one-hour response time for every support plan and every incident.

## Exclusions
Availability calculations may exclude planned maintenance, customer-controlled systems, third-party outages and other contractually defined exclusions.
