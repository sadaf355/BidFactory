# NovaTech Solutions — Enterprise Support Policy

Document ID: NTS-SUP-ENT-002
Classification: Customer Support Policy — Synthetic Demo Data
Version: 1.9

## Enterprise Support
Enterprise customers may receive 24/7 incident support for covered services.

## Support Channels
Supported channels may include customer portal, email and escalation procedures defined in the service agreement.

## Incident Management
Incidents are logged, categorized and prioritized. Critical incidents may trigger an escalation process involving technical and service management personnel.

## Service Reporting
Enterprise service reporting may include incident summaries, service trends and operational reviews.

## Limitations
Specific named personnel, dedicated support teams, response targets and restoration commitments depend on the contracted support package.
