## Engagement Model
Implementation projects are priced based on scope, delivery complexity, integrations, staffing requirements, timeline and risk.

## Illustrative Ranges
- Assessment and architecture: USD 20,000–50,000
- Application modernization: USD 75,000–300,000+
- Enterprise integration program: USD 50,000–250,000+
- Data and analytics platform: USD 60,000–250,000+

## Government and Large Enterprise Projects
Large projects may use milestone-based pricing, fixed-price work packages or time-and-materials models depending on procurement requirements.

## Commercial Controls
Final proposals require internal commercial approval. Pricing decisions must not be inferred from historical ranges when an RFP requests a binding quote.

## Resource Limitation
NovaTech does not promise unlimited implementation resources. Staffing is planned according to approved project scope and capacity.
