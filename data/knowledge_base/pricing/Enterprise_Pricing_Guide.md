## Pricing Model
NovaTech uses subscription, managed-service and custom enterprise pricing models depending on solution scope.

## Enterprise Managed Services
Illustrative monthly pricing ranges:
- Foundation Managed Service: USD 5,000–10,000 per month
- Business Managed Service: USD 10,000–25,000 per month
- Enterprise Managed Service: USD 25,000+ per month

Pricing varies based on workload size, support coverage, cloud resources, security requirements and integration complexity.

## Support
Enterprise 24/7 support may be included or priced according to the selected service package.

## Important Limitation
These figures are planning ranges, not a binding quotation. Final pricing requires scope validation and commercial approval.
