# NovaTech Solutions — Disaster Recovery and Backup Framework

Document ID: NTS-TECH-DR-003
Classification: Technical Reference — Synthetic Demo Data
Version: 1.5

## Objective
NovaTech provides backup and recovery capabilities for supported managed services and project architectures.

## Backup
Backup schedules and retention periods are configured according to service tier, architecture and customer requirements.

## Recovery
Recovery procedures are documented for supported production environments. Recovery exercises may be performed based on service criticality and contractual scope.

## Disaster Recovery Planning
High-priority services may use redundant components, geographically separated recovery options or other resilience patterns where included in the agreed architecture.

## Important Limitation
Recovery Time Objectives and Recovery Point Objectives are project-specific. NovaTech does not provide one universal RTO or RPO commitment for every customer and service.

## Testing
Recovery and restoration procedures are periodically reviewed or tested according to applicable service and operational requirements.
