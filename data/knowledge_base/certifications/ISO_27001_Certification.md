## Certification Statement
NovaTech Solutions maintains an Information Security Management System (ISMS) designed to protect company and customer information assets. NovaTech Solutions is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 for the defined scope of its ISMS.

## Scope
The scope includes cloud modernization and migration services, enterprise application development, API development and integration, managed technology services, data platforms and analytics services, and internal systems supporting customer service delivery.

## Security Control Areas
The ISMS addresses information security governance, risk assessment, identity and access management, role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, encryption, secure software development, security monitoring, vulnerability management, incident response, backup and recovery, supplier risk management and security awareness.

## Risk Management
Periodic security risk assessments are performed. Identified risks are evaluated for impact and likelihood, with treatment actions tracked through NovaTech's security governance process.

## Statement of Applicability
NovaTech maintains a Statement of Applicability covering relevant security controls and their implementation status.
