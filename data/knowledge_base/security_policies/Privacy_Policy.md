# NovaTech Solutions — Privacy and Personal Data Handling Policy

Document ID: NTS-PRIV-001
Classification: Internal Policy — Synthetic Demo Data
Version: 1.4
Effective Date: 1 February 2026
Owner: Legal and Privacy Office

## Purpose
NovaTech Solutions handles personal information according to contractual obligations, applicable legal requirements and internal privacy controls.

## Data Minimization
Personal information should be collected and processed only when necessary for a defined business or service purpose.

## Access and Confidentiality
Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel with a legitimate business need.

## Retention
Personal information is retained according to contractual, legal and operational requirements. Information is disposed of or anonymized when retention is no longer required, subject to applicable obligations.

## Customer Responsibilities
For customer-managed data and applications, customers remain responsible for defining their own lawful processing requirements and data governance obligations where contractually applicable.

## Limitations
This policy does not claim that NovaTech is universally certified under HIPAA or any other healthcare-specific regulatory framework.
