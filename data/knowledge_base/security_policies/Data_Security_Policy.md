# NovaTech Solutions — Data Security Policy

Document ID: NTS-SEC-POL-001
Classification: Internal Policy — Synthetic Demo Data
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 1 February 2026
Owner: Information Security Office

## Purpose
This policy defines baseline controls for protecting customer, employee and company information.

## Data Classification
Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted information requires appropriate access controls and approved handling procedures.

## Access Control
Access follows least-privilege and role-based access principles. Access to sensitive systems requires approved authentication controls. Privileged access is reviewed periodically.

## Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for privileged administrative access and for supported enterprise identity integrations where enabled by the service architecture.

## Encryption
Sensitive data is protected using approved encryption mechanisms while stored and while transmitted across untrusted networks. TLS is used for supported encrypted network communications.

## Security Monitoring
Security-relevant events are logged and monitored according to applicable system and service requirements. Significant events may trigger investigation and incident response procedures.

## Vulnerability Management
Systems are subject to vulnerability identification, prioritization and remediation processes based on risk and operational impact.

## Limitations
This policy does not constitute a claim of compliance with every industry-specific regulation.
